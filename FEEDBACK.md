# Mathematical feedback

Please open an Issue or email manchichi777@gmail.com. A useful report includes:

1. The version and PDF page.
2. The theorem, hypothesis, equation, or cited passage concerned.
3. The precise objection or question.
4. A proposed correction or missing condition, if available.
5. A source location or a complete argument supporting the point.

Please indicate whether the point concerns an internal proof, a definition, a source transcription, the proposed realization by IUT, or a typesetting error. A conditional theorem and the certification of its hypotheses are different questions.
